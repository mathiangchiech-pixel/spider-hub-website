    Architecture

    A stability-first
    approach to
    whitelist security

    Auth failures don't just annoy users — they kill a launch day, refund a month of sales and leak your hub to the free-rip channels. So we built the control plane the way SRE teams build payment systems: everything stateless at the edge, everything replicated, nothing that can hang your loader.

    • 41 edge regions, anycast in front

      Every check terminates at the closest point of presence and is answered from a replicated read replica. No region is authoritative for your users.

    • Stateless, idempotent verification

      Tokens are signed and verifiable on their own, so a packet loss, a cold node or a rolling deploy produces the exact same answer: 0.000% auth error rate over 12 months.

    • Offline grace windows

      Set 15 minutes or 72 hours. If a player loses connection mid-session, the loader keeps running instead of kicking them — then silently re-syncs.

    • Zero-downtime key rotation

      Rotate signing keys with a dual-active overlap. Nothing you shipped last year ever stops validating.

      F.A.Q
      
      The questions we get asked weekly
      
    
      
    
      
      
      01Do you keep a copy of my source code?
      By default, no. Your source is streamed into an ephemeral build sandbox, obfuscated, and the plaintext is shredded as soon as the build is signed. What we retain is the SHA-256 of the payload, the build manifest and the policy metadata needed to authenticate. If you switch on the encrypted vault, copies are sealed with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase only you hold — we cannot read them, and we cannot restore them for you.
      
      
      02What happens to my users if Spider Hub goes down?
      Nothing they can see. Verification tokens are self-contained, so a loader inside its grace window keeps running without contacting us at all. Set the window to 15 minutes or 72 hours per script. Auth endpoints are anycast across 41 regions, so a region failure reroutes mid-session; you get an SLA credit if we ever miss 99.99%.
      
      
      03Can a determined leaker just bypass the whitelist?
      Any client-side check can be attacked — the goal is making it more expensive than buying a key. We sign the loader, verify a build-level integrity hash server-side, score the fingerprint, bind the HWID, serve a decoy build instead of an error when tampering is detected, and expire tokens on a short TTL so a captured payload is not reusable. Combined with the per-key instance caps, mass redistribution dies in hours, not months.
      
      
      04How do I move from another whitelist service?
      Use the importer: upload a CSV of codes, expiry timestamps, HWIDs and notes, or hand us your old service's API key and we pull it. Keys keep their remaining time, existing buyers never re-register, and we run both services in shadow mode for as long as you like so you can compare the responses before switching the loader.
      
      
      05Is the obfuscation really included?
      Yes — unlimited on every paid plan, including the trial. One Luraph-class VM pass with control-flow flattening, string encryption and anti-debug is embedded at build time. Premium and above can request a hardened profile (namespace collisions, per-function key rotation, decoy control branches). We do not charge per obfuscation and we do not cap monthly builds.
      
      
      06What is HWID exactly, and are you tracking people?
      A salted hash of non-identifiable device and executor properties — never a username, never a file path, never anything a user typed. It exists so one key cannot be shared across 400 machines. The hash is only meaningful to your script, and users get a self-service reset budget so a reinstall does not become a support ticket.
      
      
      07Which executors and platforms work?
      Everything that can run HttpGet/request and a loadstring — Windows, macOS, Android and ARM builds included. The loader needs no library, no external binary and no privileged API. If your target can't make an HTTPS request, the offline grace mode plus pre-issued tokens still works; ask us and we will write the shim.
      
      
      08Do you take a cut of my ad revenue?
      No. Reward links pay you directly from the network you choose; we take 0% and we never sit in the payout path. We publish per-network completion rates so you can see why a click did not convert instead of guessing — and you can plug in your own shortener with custom URLs on Pro.
      
      
      09Is this allowed with Roblox's ToS?
      Spider Hub protects and licenses your own code; it does not inject into games, exploit other players or automate purchases. Scripting itself sits in a grey area that you and your users are responsible for, and we do not permit scripts whose purpose is stealing, trading abuse or account compromise — those are rejected from Verified mode and removed under our ToS.
      
      
      10What does the Enterprise plan actually unlock?
      Dedicated capacity and a rate-limit bypass, per-key instance caps, execution ceilings, HWID-less modes, cloned-emulator allow/blocklists, custom ad URLs on your own domain, SSO for staff, a signed DPA with uptime credits, and a direct line to the engineers who run the build pipeline for feature requests.
